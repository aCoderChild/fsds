# MUSE-News official release follow-up

Checked 2026-11-09 as a no-GPU source audit. This does not create experiment metrics.

## What the primary sources establish

- The [MUSE paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.06460) says the News target starts from Llama 2 7B and is fine-tuned on BBC articles. Its appendix says the KnowMem QAs are generated from randomly sampled 2,048-token corpus excerpts and answer strings are validated against their corresponding excerpts.
- The [MUSE-News dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/muse-bench/MUSE-News) describes 100 KnowMem forget QAs as testing the corresponding `raw/forget` split.
- The pinned [official MUSE loader](https://github.com/swj0419/muse_bench/blob/fcd6651da8bcf34744fb9a1cc69bb856ab3eb277/load_data.py) serializes KnowMem rows as only `question` and `answer`, and Verbatim rows as only `prompt` and `gt`. It does not release the excerpt/article crosswalk. The target checkpoint's autogenerated [model card](https://huggingface.co/muse-bench/MUSE-news_target) has no license or fine-tuning provenance fields filled in.

## Local release checks

- The pinned QA release has 100 forget questions. Exact normalized-answer substring search finds a candidate in `train/forget` for all 100, but finds no candidate in `raw/forget` for 26. Of those 26, 13 answer strings also occur in at least one other raw split. Across all 100 rows, answer strings occur in multiple raw splits for 59 rows. These are lexical candidate counts, not verified article links or proof of split leakage.
- The public target checkpoint's pinned file tree has six weight shards and configuration files, but no tokenizer files. The official Llama 2 tokenizer is a separate gated repository. The prior Kaggle access preflight failed inside Kaggle secret retrieval before contacting Hugging Face; it downloaded no tokenizer or model files.

## Decision

The source crosswalk cannot be recovered from the released QA rows or the official loader. Do not invent article groups for cluster-bootstrap intervals. A preregistered report of the exact official 100-row benchmark mean could be descriptive, but it would not settle the raw-split/source discrepancy or support source-cluster inference. Keep training stopped until the dataset/model provenance and applicable terms are reviewed. If the next step is an inference-only baseline, first attach a token with access to the exact official tokenizer to the MUSE Kaggle kernel and pass the hash-checked preflight; do not substitute the public mirror.
