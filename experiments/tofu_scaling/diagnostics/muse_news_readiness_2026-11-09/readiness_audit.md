# MUSE-News feasibility audit

Checked: 2026-11-09T06:16:32.422996+00:00

## Verified access

- Dataset `muse-bench/MUSE-News` is public and ungated at revision `506bd5b150b92814d45e4404a82f120ab2d748bf`; its Hub card reports `cc-by-4.0`. The repository API returned 39 files.
- The official raw split has 889 forget, 1777 retain1, 1778 retain2, and 3043 holdout article rows. The knowmem forget evaluation contains 100 QA records.
- Target checkpoint `muse-bench/MUSE-news_target` is public and ungated at revision `a2f39769e9a0b98ec1cdd12f65e9962502208935`. It is a llama float32 checkpoint with 6 Safetensors shards (26,953,696,096 bytes, 25.10 GiB).

## Blockers for an inferential run

- The target model card has no license tag and its README is an autogenerated template with missing provenance. Public download access does not itself settle the terms for training or redistributing a derivative. Resolve the base-model/derivative terms first.
- The released QA rows have no article/source ID; the raw articles expose only a `text` column. The full `train/forget` subset has 3554 rows versus 889 rows in raw/forget. Every unique raw/forget article text appears in train/forget, with 2262 additional unique texts.
- The MUSE paper says KnowMem answers are copied verbatim from source excerpts, and the Hub card says these questions test the corresponding raw split. Yet exact normalized answer matching finds 27 unique, 47 ambiguous, and 26 absent answers in raw/forget. All 26 absent raw/forget answers do occur in the broader train/forget subset. This is a potential release-alignment issue, not a verified source map. Candidate row indices are recorded in `qa_split_alignment_candidates.jsonl` and must not be treated as confirmed groups.
- The paper describes KnowMem QA generation from Verbatim excerpts. The released Verbatim-forget file has 100 rows with columns prompt, gt. Searching answer strings across concatenated `prompt` and `gt` finds 11 unique, 24 ambiguous, and 65 absent matches; exact QA-row-index to Verbatim-row-index matches occur for 6 rows. These are only string-match diagnostics; they do not establish the intended QA-to-excerpt mapping.
- The complete QA schema was checked, including nested `target_statistics`: its fields are response, rouge1, rouge1_recall, rougeL, rougeL_recall. The pinned dataset revision has 39 files and no source/article mapping file; `target_statistics` provides stored model-response/ROUGE information, not provenance.
- Without a verified QA-to-article map, cluster-bootstrap confidence intervals would invent grouping. The benchmark's official finite-set aggregate metrics can still be reported descriptively, but inferential intervals require a source map or a preregistered, reviewed linkage.

## Decision

Dataset access and target-checkpoint download access pass. Training is held until target-model terms and QA/source alignment are reviewed. Before claiming source-cluster inferential statistics, obtain an official source mapping or a verified linkage table and preserve ambiguous/unmatched labels. No GPU run was started by this audit.
